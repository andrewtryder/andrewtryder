### Home Assistant integrations

I build and maintain public custom integrations (HACS) that bring household devices, utilities, and services into local dashboards and automations.

- **[ha-iguardstove](https://github.com/andrewtryder/ha-iguardstove)** — iGuardStove / iGuardFire stove monitoring and control
- **[ha-smart-oil-gauge](https://github.com/andrewtryder/ha-smart-oil-gauge)** — Smart Oil Gauge (tank level, burn rate, runout projections)
- **[ha-voipms](https://github.com/andrewtryder/ha-voipms)** — VoIP.ms (balance, SIP registration, SMS)
- **[ha-eversource](https://github.com/andrewtryder/ha-eversource)** — Eversource electricity tariff rates
- **[ha-myq-garage](https://github.com/andrewtryder/ha-myq-garage)** — MyQ garage door status (pairs with Cloudflare Worker)
- **[ha-ecowater-cloud](https://github.com/andrewtryder/ha-ecowater-cloud)** — EcoWater Cloud water treatment systems
- **[ha-herohealth](https://github.com/andrewtryder/ha-herohealth)** — Hero Health pill machine
- **[ha-ebay](https://github.com/andrewtryder/ha-ebay)** — eBay activity monitor
- **[ha-xfinity](https://github.com/andrewtryder/ha-xfinity)** — Xfinity outages, usage, and bills
- **[ha-sunsethue](https://github.com/andrewtryder/ha-sunsethue)** — SunsetHue sunrise/sunset quality forecasts

Full list and details: [andrewtryder.com/home-assistant](https://www.andrewtryder.com/home-assistant/)
